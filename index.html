What this demo is and is not
  • Everything runs in your browser. Role scoping here is a UI simulation; production needs server-side enforcement, SSO/MFA and a database.
  • Default data is synthetic and generated from a fixed seed. It is not company performance.
  • Models: naive, 3-month moving average, seasonal naive, additive Holt-Winters. Selection uses rolling-origin backtests (6-month horizon) and MASE, with seasonal naive as the baseline. No random splits.
  • Intervals are empirical backtest residual quantiles and appear only with at least 8 backtest origins. Coverage is not separately assessed.
  • Net sales are never summed across currencies. Units are the only volume measure here.
  • Not included: Excel/PDF/OCR ingestion, malware scanning, FX conversion, reconciliation, exports to Excel/PDF, mandatory adjustment reasons, monitoring, and any certification.
